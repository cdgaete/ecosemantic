`ecosemantic-relay` holds the tunnel between a hosted bridge and an engine: the messages its text frames carry — the engine's hello, the bridge's welcome, a request's and a response's start, and a stream's reset — the binary frames that carry a stream's body in chunks of at most 64 KiB, the document the bridge publishes at its well-known address with what an engine registers and is registered as, and the Ed25519 proof a bridged request carries with the JWKs the bridge publishes to check it. The bridge service imports it for both sides of the tunnel, and `ecosemantic` depends on it for `ecosemantic serve --bridge`.
